# Portfolio Identity Kit

This identity kit establishes the design system and aesthetic rules for the project presentation, notebooks, and final capstone report.

## Design Decisions

### 1. Typography
- **Heading Font:** **Outfit** (Google Fonts) — A modern, geometric sans-serif that lends a structured, technical, and premium feel to headings.
- **Body Font:** **Inter** (Google Fonts) — A highly legible, neutral sans-serif designed for high readability in dense data layouts and technical analysis.

---

### 2. Color Palette
A clean, low-noise palette tailored to make the charts and analytical results the focal point of every page.

| Role | Color | Hex Code | Visual Preview |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Background** | Near-White | `#F8F9FA` | Warm, soft off-white to reduce eye strain |
| **Text** | Near-Black | `#1A1D20` | Deep slate charcoal for comfortable contrast |
| **Primary Brand** | Slate Blue | `#3B82F6` | Calm, professional corporate blue for main elements |
| **Accent** | Emerald Green | `#10B981` | Safe accent green for callouts, metrics, and highlights |

---

### 3. Logo / Favicon

### 4. Two-Line Style Note
Add this snippet to your project instructions to ensure consistency across all visual builds:
> **Style Note:** Headings in **Outfit**, body in **Inter**. Palette: `#F8F9FA` (bg), `#1A1D20` (text), `#3B82F6` (brand), and `#10B981` (accent).
> **Mood:** A minimalist, hyper-clean technical aesthetic designed to frame data and research clearly without competing visual clutter.
