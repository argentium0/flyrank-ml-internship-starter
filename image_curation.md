## 2. Discrimination Log: AI vs. Real Choices

- **Where Real Captures Were Chosen Over AI:**
  - **The Work (Screenshots/Visualizations):** All code, SQL results, and performance graphs are real captures of the project. Generating mock charts or fake interfaces with AI was strictly avoided to maintain complete credibility and transparency.
  - **Identity (Profile Photo):** A real photo is used for the author profile. AI-generated avatars look synthetic and reduce trust.
- **Where AI Was Used (Branding/Connective Tissue):**
  - AI was used only to generate the minimalist vector background texture and favicon monogram, ensuring the branding matches the style guide.

---

## 3. Rejected Assets & Rationale (discernment)

- **Rejected Asset 1: 3D Glossy Monogram Logo (AI-Generated)**
  - *Why it was rejected:* The generator produced a glossy, shaded 3D chrome letter "A" on a dark glass button. While visually flashy, it looked generic and clashed with the flat, technical, minimalist design system of the portfolio.
- **Rejected Asset 2: Mock Code Interface Graphic (AI-Generated)**
  - *Why it was rejected:* An abstract illustration of floating code lines and database cylinders was tested for the technical sections. It was rejected because it looked like stock marketing fluff. A clean, cropped capture of the actual DuckDB query is infinitely more convincing.
